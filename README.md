## About Me

I am an Anatomy graduate from Babcock University, Nigeria (**B.Sc., CGPA 4.33/5.00**), developing the experimental and computational foundation to study how ion channels, membrane receptors, and intracellular signaling networks influence cellular behavior in health and disease.

My undergraduate thesis combined **RT-qPCR, histology, molecular docking, ADMET profiling, protein-protein interaction mapping, and statistical analysis** to investigate neurotrophic and inflammatory gene expression in the hippocampus of *Escherichia coli*-inoculated Wistar rats treated with *Bryophyllum pinnatum* extract.

I am now documenting my progression from guided programming exercises to reproducible biological analyses in preparation for research training in molecular genetics.

## Research Focus

| Theme | Questions That Interest Me |
|---|---|
| **Ion channels and membrane receptors** | How do altered channel and receptor states influence cancer-cell behavior and therapeutic response? |
| **Cell signaling** | How do extracellular cues propagate through intracellular cascades into transcriptional responses? |
| **Structural biology** | How can the structures of channels and receptors reveal selective therapeutic vulnerabilities? |
| **Computational genomics** | How can public sequencing and expression data expose disease mechanisms and test biological hypotheses? |
| **Signal-driven transcription** | How do changes at the membrane ultimately reprogram cell identity and gene expression? |

## Documentation Standard

My private learning journal remains personal and intentionally unpolished. Public repositories contain curated evidence that another learner or researcher can inspect and reproduce.

Each public investigation should document:

1. A specific biological question and hypothesis
2. Data source, accession, and retrieval date
3. Reproducible methods and software versions
4. Quality control and analytical choices
5. Results with original figures or tables
6. Biological interpretation without overstating conclusions
7. Limitations, failed attempts, and next steps

## Approach

```text
Question → Investigate → Fail → Debug → Validate → Interpret → Document → Repeat
```

The goal is not to appear finished. It is to make progress visible, reproducible, and scientifically honest.
